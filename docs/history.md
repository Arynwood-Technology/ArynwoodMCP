## From Community to Grove

Arynwood Grove is a separate, non-AI community service. Its first commit, `8aa6fbd`, is dated
2026-09-23, “Initial Arynwood Community release”. Community 0.3 upgrades in place.
The v0.4.0 tag dates to 2026-10-08; v0.7.3 was released on 2026-10-23.
[Its notes](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/arynwood-community/blob/main/docs/PRERELEASE.md)
cover 0.3.0–0.7.3; 0.3.0, 0.5.0 and 0.7.0 have notes but no tag.
