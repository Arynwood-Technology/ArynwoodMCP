# Arynwood MCP 0.5.1: managing Community spaces

**Status: release candidate.** Packages are built by the tagged release workflows.

Anyone on 0.4.8 or later sees the update notice once this is published; earlier versions need
one manual install.

## What's new

**Manage a space.** A space's owners get **Manage space** on the Community page:

- Rename the space and give it a topic, which every member sees under its name. Open pages
  show the change within about a second.
- Delete the space for everyone, after typing its name. Members who have it open move to
  another space and are told why.
- Make other members owners, or members again, and remove people. A space always keeps at
  least one owner, so the only owner can neither step down nor leave.

**Assign tasks from the Calendar.** The Calendar's task form has "Assigned to", as Tasks
already did, and each task shows who has it. A private task, which only you can see, can be
assigned only to you.

These need Arynwood Grove 0.4.2 or later. With an older Grove, the new actions say the Grove
needs updating; everything else works as before.

- Grove 0.4.2: 56 server tests (new: roles and the one-owner rule, deletion removing
  everything, live notices for renames and roles, deletion ending members' live notices, a

  by the other owner reaching the app live, a task assigned from the Calendar, and deleting the
  space, all passed.
- Through the relay, an older Grove's "Method Not Allowed" reaches the page, which shows the
  update message.
